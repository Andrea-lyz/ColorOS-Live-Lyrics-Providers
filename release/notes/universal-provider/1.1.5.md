通用播放器 Provider 1.1.5

- TTML 与对唱：Apple Music 的歌词以 TTML 获取并附带原文，Bridge 5.1.0 据此把对唱歌词分到左右两侧；翻译仍随普通翻译发布，翻译开关照常生效。
- 修复 Apple Music 来源：原接口（paxsenix）已持续不可用，改为从 Better Lyrics 读取已缓存的逐字 TTML。它只免费提供已缓存的歌曲，未缓存的会视为没有结果，转由下一个来源查找；paxsenix 保留为最后的备用。
- 新增 Apple Music 官方歌词（可选，默认关闭）：在 App 内登录 music.apple.com 后，直接读取官方 TTML（含对唱与翻译）。需要有效的 Apple Music 订阅（含试用）；无法获取官方歌词时会尝试免费来源；“账号没有订阅”是根据歌词及账号接口响应作出的推断，也可能受曲库地区或服务状态影响。登录凭据只保存在本机，只交给系统进程，不写入日志；凭据过期时打开 App 会尝试用保存的登录状态自动续期。曲库地区默认跟随账号，也可手动指定。
- 新增酷狗音乐来源：逐字歌词与翻译。已保存过来源顺序的用户需要在“歌词源优先级”中手动开启。
- QQ 音乐：增加两个搜索入口作为备用（部分地区主接口返回空结果时仍能搜到），搜索结果包含同一首歌的其他版本，歌词请求补充歌手、专辑与时长信息；修复部分逐字歌词的单字时长为 0 时一闪而过。
- 新增“TTML 优先”（默认关闭）：先查能直接给出 TTML 的来源（Apple Music）；都没有时把其他格式转换为 TTML 发布。转换的歌词不含演唱者，不会出现对唱分侧。
- 首页、最近记录和搜索结果显示歌词的真实格式，例如“QQ音乐 · QRC”“酷狗音乐 · KRC→TTML”“Apple Music · TTML”。搜索结果会先取一次歌词来确定格式，点击时直接使用；免费的 Apple Music 结果不预先获取。
- 已登录订阅账号的官方歌词获取尚未进行真机验证；登录和未订阅账号的回退已用调试包测试。
- 同一播放器只开一个 Provider：通用播放器 Provider 与该播放器的专属 Provider 二选一，同时开启会互相覆盖歌词。

Universal Player Provider 1.1.5

- TTML and duets: Apple Music lyrics are fetched as TTML and the document is attached, so Bridge 5.1.0 can place duet lines on the side of their singer. Translations still travel in the regular translation lane, and the translation switch works as before.
- Fixes the Apple Music source: the previous endpoint (paxsenix) has been down for a long time, so word-timed TTML now comes from Better Lyrics' cache. It only serves cached songs for free; an uncached song counts as no result and the next source is asked. paxsenix stays as the last fallback.
- New, optional (off by default) official Apple Music lyrics: after signing in to music.apple.com inside the app, the official TTML (with duets and translations) is read directly. This needs an active Apple Music subscription (or trial); when official lyrics cannot be fetched the app tries the free sources. Its “no subscription” status is inferred from the lyrics and account responses and can also be affected by storefront or service issues. The sign-in token stays on the phone, is handed only to the system process and is never logged; when it expires, opening the app tries to renew it from the saved web session. The storefront follows the account by default and can be set by hand.
- New KuGou source with word-timed lyrics and translations. If you saved a source order before, turn it on in "Lyric source priority".
- QQ Music: two more search entry points as fallbacks (for regions where the main endpoint returns nothing), search results include other versions of the same song, and lyric requests carry the artist, album and duration. Fixes single words with a zero duration flashing past.
- New "TTML first" switch (off by default): asks sources that deliver TTML (Apple Music) first, and otherwise converts the lyric to TTML. Converted lyrics name no singers, so they never split into a duet.
- Home, recent history and search results show the lyric's real format, for example "QQ Music · QRC", "KuGou · KRC→TTML" or "Apple Music · TTML". Search results fetch each lyric once to find its format and reuse it when tapped; free Apple Music results are not fetched in advance.
- Official lyric retrieval using a subscribed account has not been verified on a device; sign-in and the unsubscribed-account fallback were checked using debug builds.
- Enable only one Provider per player: the Universal Player Provider or that player's dedicated Provider, not both, or they overwrite each other's lyrics.
