动态封面 Provider 1.0.7

- 计费网络（未开启“允许计费网络下载”）或离线时，已下载并核验过的动态封面在缓存映射过期后 30 天内仍可显示，不再因 24 小时到期而退回静态封面。网络允许时照旧重新核验。
- 命中缓存会刷新其索引时间，常听歌曲的索引不再按写入顺序被先淘汰。
- 诊断新增：已保存的绑定没有命中当前歌曲时记录原因（未填专辑、专辑未绑定、歌手不符，只记原因码）；环境记录补充网络是否已验证、是否计费、是否允许计费网络。

Dynamic Artwork Provider 1.0.7

- On a metered network (with "Allow metered downloads" off) or offline, a motion cover that was already downloaded and verified keeps showing for up to 30 days after its cache mapping expires, instead of falling back to the static cover after 24 hours. When the network is allowed it is re-verified as before.
- A cache hit refreshes its index time, so frequently played songs are no longer evicted first by write order.
- Diagnostics: when saved bindings do not match the playing song, the reason is recorded (album missing, album not bound, artist not bound; reason codes only); the environment line adds whether the network is validated, metered and allowed.
