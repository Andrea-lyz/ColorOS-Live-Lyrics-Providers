QQ音乐 Provider 1.2.4

- 修复部分歌曲锁屏不显示翻译：当歌曲的翻译行数与原文不一致时（例如「词：」「曲：」等署名行没有翻译），QQ 音乐会在交出歌词前直接丢弃整份翻译。现在插件会在 QQ 丢弃之前保留翻译，并按时间与原文逐行对齐；没有翻译的署名行保持空白，不会错位。
- QQ 音乐 20.7 与 21.0 均适用；翻译行数一致的歌曲行为与 1.2.3 相同。

QQ Music Provider 1.2.4

- Fixes missing lock-screen translations on some songs: when a song's translation has a different line count from the lyric (for example, credit lines such as "词：" / "曲：" have no translation), QQ Music discards the whole translation before handing the lyric over. The module now keeps the translation before QQ discards it and aligns it to the lyric by time; untranslated credit lines stay blank instead of shifting the translations.
- Works on QQ Music 20.7 and 21.0; songs whose translation line count already matches behave as in 1.2.3.
