QQ音乐 Provider 1.2.3

- 修复 QQ 音乐 21.0 起锁屏不显示歌词翻译：新版 QQ 音乐调整了内部方法名，插件取到的翻译实际上是歌曲信息，因此始终为空。现在改为读取不随版本变化的字段，QQ 音乐 20.7 与 21.0 均可正常取到翻译。
- 修复 QQ 音乐 21.0 上同一首歌被反复识别为“新歌曲”：歌曲 ID、歌名与歌手同样改为兼容新旧版本的读取方式。
- 其余歌词获取与发布逻辑与 1.2.2 相同。

QQ Music Provider 1.2.3

- Fixes lock-screen translations missing since QQ Music 21.0: the new QQ Music renamed internal methods, so the module read song info in place of the translation and always got nothing. It now reads fields that do not change between versions, and translations work on both QQ Music 20.7 and 21.0.
- Fixes the same song being detected as a new track over and over on QQ Music 21.0: song ID, title and artist are now read in a way that works on both old and new versions.
- Other lyric fetching and publishing is unchanged from 1.2.2.
